Dimension Seven Systems Website Build Instructions

Build a polished, responsive marketing website for Dimension Seven Systems.

The site should feel technical and credible, but still accessible to a business owner. Use the attached screenshot as the visual reference for color, typography, spacing, cards, buttons and overall atmosphere.

Do not copy the screenshot’s branding or content. Adapt its visual system to Dimension Seven Systems.

1. Business Positioning

Dimension Seven Systems builds conversion systems.

D7 operates between lead generation and the final sale. It helps businesses convert more of the leads, traffic and opportunities they already have.

D7 is not positioned as:

A lead-generation agency
An AI agency
An automation agency
A generic web design company
A CRM reseller

The site should present D7 as the company that identifies conversion leaks then builds and connects the components needed to fix them.

The main visitor journey is:

Recognize the leaks → Understand the system → See how D7 works → Review proof → Book a Conversion Review

Copy is not the focus yet. Use clean placeholders where final copy is unavailable. Do not use generic agency filler such as “elevate your brand” or “unlock your potential.”

2. Visual Direction

Match the visual language of the screenshot:

Dark, nearly black background
White display headings
Muted blue-gray body copy
Bright blue primary accent
Cyan and turquoise secondary accents
Thin blue-gray borders
Dark elevated cards
Soft glows used sparingly
Large, confident typography
Generous whitespace
Product-interface visuals
Slight technical or systems-oriented feel
Minimal animation

The website should look custom-built. Avoid the usual SaaS template appearance with excessive gradients, floating blobs or dozens of identical cards.

12. Motion

Use motion to explain the system, not decorate the page.

Allowed:

Slow status pulse
Lines illuminating as a lead moves through the system
Cards entering with subtle opacity and vertical movement
Count-up metrics when visible
Accordion transitions
Button hover feedback
Small diagram-state changes

Avoid:

Parallax
Constant floating
Rotating objects
Large animated gradients
Aggressive cursor effects
Long entrance animations

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

13. Responsive Behavior

Required breakpoints:

--mobile: 480px;
--tablet: 768px;
--desktop: 1024px;
--wide: 1280px;

Requirements:

No horizontal page overflow
Minimum 44px tap targets
Mobile body text of at least 16px
Hero becomes one column below tablet width
Navigation becomes a menu button
Diagrams remain readable on narrow screens
Tables scroll inside their own container
Buttons can stack at small widths
Cards collapse without losing hierarchy
Video maintains a 16:9 ratio
Forms use one column on mobile

No important information conveyed by color alone

18. Quality Standard

Before considering the build complete:

Verify every breakpoint
Test all navigation
Test keyboard behavior
Test form validation
Check focus states
Confirm there is no horizontal overflow
Run Lighthouse
Check contrast
Check reduced motion
Verify all placeholder content is clearly identified
Remove fake testimonials and invented performance numbers
Confirm every CTA has a real destination or a clearly documented placeholder
Make sure the design still feels intentional with incomplete content

The final result should feel like a company that understands systems, revenue and operational leaks. It should not look like a generic marketing agency or another AI automation startup.
